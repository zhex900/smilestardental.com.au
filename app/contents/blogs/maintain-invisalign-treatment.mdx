Consistent wear and hygiene make the biggest difference with clear aligners.

## Wear time

Aim for **20–22 hours per day**, removing trays only for meals and brushing.

## Cleaning

Rinse trays when you take them out; clean daily with products your dentist recommends—avoid hot water that can warp plastic.

## Check-ins

Attend scheduled appointments so progress can be tracked and refinements planned if needed.
