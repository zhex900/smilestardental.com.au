Custom oral appliances advance the lower jaw slightly to help keep the airway open during sleep. They are not a DIY solution—diagnosis and follow-up matter.

## Who might benefit

Adults with mild to moderate obstructive sleep apnea, or those who cannot tolerate CPAP, may be candidates after a sleep study and physician direction. Primary snoring without apnea needs careful evaluation so serious sleep disorders are not missed.

## Fitting and adjustment

Dentists take impressions or scans for a lab-fabricated device, then adjust protrusion over visits for comfort and effectiveness. Jaw discomfort or tooth movement risks are discussed upfront.

## Ongoing care

Appliances need cleaning, periodic replacement, and repeat sleep testing when your doctor recommends it to confirm treatment is still working.
