Fluoride is one of the most studied tools in dentistry. In the right amounts, it makes enamel more resistant to acid from bacteria and sugar.

## Community water and toothpaste

Many areas have optimally fluoridated tap water. For children, use a smear (rice-sized) of fluoride toothpaste until about age three, then a pea-sized amount—always supervise brushing to minimise swallowing.

## Professional treatments

Dentists may apply fluoride varnish at visits for children at higher cavity risk. This is quick, safe, and repeated based on individual need—not a one-size-fits-all schedule.

## Fluorosis vs cavities

Mild fluorosis is cosmetic; severe decay affects eating, sleep, and school. Your dentist can balance risk and tailor guidance if you use well water or multiple fluoride sources.
