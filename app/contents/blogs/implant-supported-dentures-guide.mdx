Implant-supported dentures clip or snap onto attachments connected to implants in the jaw. They still come out for cleaning but stay far more secure than conventional dentures alone.

## Two-implant overdenture (lower jaw)

The lower denture often benefits most from two implants in the front—speech and chewing improve for many wearers with less adhesive mess.

## More implants, more options

Additional implants can support a stronger bar or even a fixed hybrid bridge that only your dentist removes for maintenance.

## Candidacy and bone

Adequate bone volume (or grafting), healthy gums, and good overall health factor into planning. Healing phases vary; temporary teeth may be worn during integration.

Your team should explain cleaning, recall visits, and long-term component wear so expectations stay realistic.
