A wand-sized camera captures thousands of images per second and stitches them into an accurate 3D model of your teeth and gums.

## Patient experience

Many people gag on traditional impressions. Scanning is often faster and can be paused if you need a break. The result is instantly visible on screen.

## Clinical advantages

Labs and milling machines use the same digital file for crowns, bridges, aligners, and surgical guides—reducing remakes and shipping delays. Your dentist can show simulations and explain treatment more clearly.

## Limitations

Some cases still need conventional records or a combination approach. Your provider will choose the method that fits your anatomy and treatment plan.
