Professional whitening differs from trends you see online. Here is a concise myth-vs-fact view.

## Myth: Whitening damages enamel permanently

**Fact:** When supervised and product instructions are followed, professional options are designed to minimize sensitivity and protect enamel compared with unregulated DIY mixes.

## Myth: All stains respond the same

**Fact:** Yellowing from aging or diet often responds well; some intrinsic stains may need veneers or other options—your dentist can classify yours.

## Myth: Results last forever without care

**Fact:** Diet, smoking, and oral hygiene affect longevity; touch-ups and good habits extend results.
